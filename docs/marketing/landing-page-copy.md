# Pyxie — Landing Page Copy (Waitlist)

Single-page waitlist landing. Five-second test: *What is this? Who is it for? What do I do?* — answered by the hero alone.

Audience: Gen Z (16–24), gym-curious, motivation-starved, Pokémon/Tamagotchi-literate. Goal: email capture.

Voice anchor: **wry, honest, warm — in that order.** See `brand-guide.md`.

---

> # Your pet eats your workouts.
>
> Skip leg day and it gets hungry. Skip a week and it dies — for real, no second chances. A virtual pet that lives or dies by whether you actually move today.
>
> **[ Claim a starter egg → ]**     [ See how it works ]

**Why this works:** The headline is the entire pitch in five words. Subhead names the stakes and the audience in the same breath. CTA is concrete — you're not "joining a waitlist," you're claiming the thing the product is about.

## SECTION 2 — How it works

Three steps. Headline + one sentence each. No more.

> ## How Pyxie works
>
> **1. Pick a starter.**
> Ember, Tide, or Verdant. Permanent for that pet's life — choose like you mean it.
>
> **2. Train.**
> Log a workout, your pet eats. Easy walk or hard session — both count, but they grow different creatures.
>
> **3. Don't stop.**
> Miss a day, stats decay. Miss two, your pet starves. Miss enough, it's gone, and you start over with a new egg.

## SECTION 3 — The hook (permadeath section)

The strongest reason to scroll past the hero. Give it room.

> ## There are no second chances.
>
> Most fitness apps give you streaks you can lose, badges you can re-earn, and "let's get back on track!" emails when you slip.
>
> Pyxie doesn't.
>
> If your pet dies, it's gone. You release the body, hatch a new egg, and start at stage zero with no record of the pet that was. The streak you built is buried with it.
>
> That sounds harsh. It is. It's also the part that finally makes you go to the gym.

## SECTION 5 — What it isn't

Differentiation through exclusion. Sharper than a feature grid for a consumer product at waitlist stage.

> ## A few things Pyxie won't do.
>
> - **No macros, no meal plans, no "fitness journey."** If you wanted a coach, you'd already have one.
> - **No "you got this!" notifications.** Your pet sends its own. They're harder to ignore.
> - **No buying your way back to a dead pet.** The dead stay dead. That's the whole deal.
> - **No ads, no sponsored evolutions, no NFTs.** Just one egg, your reps, and what happens next.

---

## SECTION 6 — FAQ (short)

Three questions. The ones that will actually come up. Keep it tight.

> ## Quick questions
>
> **Wait, the pet can actually die?**
> Yes. Two days without food and it's in critical condition. A few more and it's gone. You can hatch another egg whenever you're ready — but it'll be a new pet, not the old one back.
>
> **Does it sync with my smartwatch?**
> At launch: manual logging plus Apple Health and Google Fit. Strava, Garmin, and Whoop on the roadmap. Whatever you log counts — Pyxie doesn't care which app caught the workout.
>
> **Is this free?**
> Free to hatch, free to play, free to lose your pet. We'll have an optional supporter tier later for people who want to keep the lights on. No pay-to-revive, ever.
