# Pyxie Roster Catalog

Every named creature in the Pyxie evolution tree. Eight elemental lines, 31 forms per line, **248 total.**

This is a horizon, not a checklist. Most playthroughs see fewer than ten of these. You can't trade for what you didn't hatch, and you can't reroll a line you didn't get. The catalog exists so you can see what you're not getting — that's part of the deal.

---

## How to read this

Each row is a form. The **ID** column shows the lineage path:

- The first segment is the line (`ember`, `tide`, etc.).
- The dash-separated suffix is the evolution path: a sequence of **`p`** (primary) and **`a`** (alt) characters, one per evolution since the baseline.
- `ember-pap` reads "Ember → primary → alt → primary." The path is decided by your workout pattern at each stage, not by you.

Stages run 0 (baseline / freshly hatched) through 4 (terminal final form). Final-stage IDs are 4 characters of path.

Branching axes:
- Stage 0→1, Stage 2→3: **intensity** (easy/medium = primary; hard = alt)
- Stage 1→2, Stage 3→4: **complexity** (beginner = primary; intermediate/advanced = alt)

## The math, for the curious

Per line:
- 1 baseline + 2 stage-1 + 4 stage-2 + 8 stage-3 + 16 stage-4 = **31 forms**

Across 8 lines:
- 31 × 8 = **248 forms**
- 16 × 8 = **128 final-stage forms**

Average pets per playthrough (lifetime expectation, rough): one egg, raised to stage 5 if you're disciplined. Most players will see between **5 and 30** distinct named forms across all the pets they ever raise. The remaining 218+ live in the catalog and never come out.

That's the deal.
